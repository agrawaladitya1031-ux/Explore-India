Major Festivals of India

India is often called the "Land of Festivals". Here is a chronological list of major celebrations:

  1. Diwali: The festival of lights celebrating the victory of light over darkness.
  2. Holi: The festival of colors marking the arrival of spring.
  3. Eid-ul-Fitr: Marks the end of the holy month of Ramadan.
  4. Onam: The harvest festival of Kerala featuring boat races and grand feasts.
  5. Christmas: Celebrated with joy, light decorations, and carols across the country.

Classical Dance Forms

India has 8 major classical dance styles recognized nationally:

  • Bharatanatyam – Tamil Nadu
  • Kathak – Northern India
  • Kathakali – Kerala
  • Odissi – Odisha
  • Manipuri – Manipur
