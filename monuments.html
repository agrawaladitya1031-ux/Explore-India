Wonders of Architecture

India's architectural heritage spans thousands of years. Here are key monuments built in the 16th and 17th centuries:

1. Taj Mahal (Agra)
Built by Emperor Shah Jahan in memory of Mumtaz Mahal. Made of pure white marble, it covers an area exceeding 170,000 m2.

2. Qutub Minar (Delhi)
A 73-meter tall minaret constructed in the 12th century. It is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

3. Red Fort (Delhi)
Constructed during the reign of Shah Jahan in the mid-17th century, famous for its red sandstone walls.

4. Gateway of India (Mumbai)
Erected to commemorate the landing of King George V and Queen Mary in 1911.
