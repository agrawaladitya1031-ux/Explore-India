Regional Specialties

Indian food is celebrated around the globe for its sophisticated use of spices like turmeric, cardamom, and cumin.

Popular Dishes by Region:

  • North India:
    • Paneer Butter Masala
    • Butter Naan & Dal Makhani
  • South India:
    • Masala Dosa with Sambar
    • Idli & Coconut Chutney
  • East & West India:
    • Rosogolla (West Bengal)
    • Pav Bhaji (Maharashtra)
